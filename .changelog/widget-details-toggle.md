- **Footer widget details toggle (`widget.details`)** — `widget.details=false` in `~/.pi-lens/config.json` (or `--no-widget-details`) collapses the below-editor widget to its single headline line — current outstanding counts (`●E`/`!W`) or `✓ clean` — hiding the file rows, the suppressed count, and the blocking-diagnostic detail lines. Details render by default; the toggle is for users who want a minimal footer. Resolved at session start.
